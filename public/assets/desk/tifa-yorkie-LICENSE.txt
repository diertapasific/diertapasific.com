The creator's bow-free scan has been simplified from 523,626 to 65,000
triangles, rotated to Y-up, centered, resized, and converted from STL to
GLB for this portfolio. Normals were recomputed and smoothed. Monochrome coat shading, separate
eyes and nose, blinking, breathing, and weighted head movement are applied
at runtime. The source has no
animation rig. No endorsement by the original creator is implied.

The original creator's attribution and license apply to this adapted mesh.
